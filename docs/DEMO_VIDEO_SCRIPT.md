# MarketLink demo video script

Aim for 6–8 minutes. Record at 1920×1080, then a phone-width pass on the product page and cart.

1. Homepage (30s). Show the hero, featured markets, farmers, and produce. Toggle dark mode.
2. Markets (40s). Filter by Saturday, pan the Leaflet map, open a marker popup, and open directions.
3. Products (50s). Search “honey”, filter by category and price, sort by rating, and open a product. Add one item to the cart.
4. Customer account (90s). Log in as customer@marketlink.com. Show favorites, notifications, and the cart. Place a pre-order for a date three days out. Open the order timeline and the printable invoice.
5. Farmer account (90s). Log in as farmer@marketlink.com. Accept the new order, mark it ready, then complete an older order. Show low-stock items, the weekly stock buttons, insights chart, and a review reply.
6. Admin (70s). Log in as admin@marketlink.com. Show the charts, approve or leave the pending Shah stall, open reports, and export CSV. Publish a short announcement and confirm it on the homepage.
7. Chat and contact (30s). Ask the chatbot “How do I pre-order?” and show the contact map.
8. Close (15s). Restate that payment is in person and there is no delivery.

Narration line: “MarketLink is the pickup board for the market, not a checkout.”
